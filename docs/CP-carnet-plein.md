# CP · CARNET PLEIN : les Fanzzy de fin de carnet, un par PASS

*6 novembre 2026, fil « Créer un Fanzzy récompense de fin de PASS ». Validé
par Gaël (« Oui, ça me va », les 4 noms gardés, « les 4 d'un coup »).
Génération en mode rapide gratuit seulement (Unlimited, Nano Banana 2, 2K,
1 variante ; clips Veo 3.1 Lite en Unlimited comme TB1), 0 crédit.*

Hors-série **CARNET PLEIN** (code `CP`, `src/shared/fanzzy/dex-carnet-plein.js`) :
jamais dans un booster ; chacun se gagne au dernier palier du carnet de sa
saison, avec ou sans PASS. Couleurs de la série : or `#F2C14E`, brun `#2B1A0E`.
Méthode commune : `00-METHODE.md` (légendaire : un mythe, une silhouette
iconique, une couronne, la lumière d'or collée au corps, des matières nobles).
Tous ont un **petit médaillon d'or en forme de carnet tamponné** sur la
poitrine : c'est la marque de la série.

| ID | Nom | PASS | Type | Geste du cri | Cri | Fond |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CP1 | Le Gardien du Feu | 1 Préhistorique | pyro | meche | LE FEU NE S'ÉTEINT PAS | vert |
| CP2 | Le Ferrailleur | 2 Apocalyptique | perc | salves | TOUT SE RÉPARE | vert |
| CP3 | Le Capitaine Comète | 3 Spatiale | depl | cortege | CAP SUR LA VICTOIRE | vert |
| CP4 | Le Fantôme du Virage | 4 Halloween | fide | retenue | ON NE PART JAMAIS | vert |

Tenues : base, halloween, prehistorique, apocalyptique (spatiale plus tard,
quand la tenue aura ses premières images) ; une Ultimate par tenue, comme TB1.

Prompt de la carte : celui de TB1 (`fanzzy-prompts/TB-thebestfan.md`), en
remplaçant le paragraphe d'identité, la ligne « Theme: » (= ligne Légendaire)
et la pose par ceux de la fiche ci-dessous. Fond vert #00B140.

## CP1 · Le Gardien du Feu · légendaire · fond vert

**Mythe : le supporter qui a porté la braise du premier virage à travers
tout l'hiver.**

```
IDENTITÉ: A sturdy, broad-shouldered supporter with a kind weathered face, a short braided reddish-brown beard and warm amber eyes, his hair tied back. He wears a sleeveless tunic of thick brown hide over a dark red knitted jumper, a wide leather belt with a bone buckle, and leather wraps on his forearms and shins. Around his neck, falling to his knees, a long hand-knitted football supporter's bar scarf in deep red (#B3261E) and gold (#F2C14E) bars, plain knit with no text and no crest. On his chest, a small round gold medallion shaped like a stamped card. His signature item is a tall wooden torch-staff topped with a burning ember held in a cage of bone, held in one hand: keep exactly these colours.
```

- Légendaire : `The keeper of the first stand's fire turned into a living legend: his hide tunic now edged with gold leaf, the scarf woven with fine gold threads, the torch-staff of dark polished wood ringed with gold, its ember glowing bright orange inside a cage of carved ivory. Crowning element: a crown of tall stylised golden flames rising behind his head and shoulders, readable at thumbnail size. Golden light (#FFC23D) glowing from the ember and along the gold edges, staying on the body. His feet on a small round hearth stone ringed with gold. Calm, proud, protective face, mouth closed.`
- Pose : `Standing tall facing the viewer, the torch-staff held upright at his side in one hand, the other hand open on his chest over the medallion, proud protective expression, mouth closed. Correct anatomy: exactly two arms, two legs, two hands with five fingers each, no extra or duplicated limbs.`
- Planche, figure « pousse » : `raising the torch-staff high above his head, close to his body, shouting the chant`
- Skins :
  - Halloween : `A jack-o'-lantern fire keeper: the ember now a small glowing carved pumpkin in the bone cage, a tattered purple and orange cape, stitched bats on the scarf.`
  - Préhistorique : `The first fire of the cave: rough mammoth fur over the shoulders, bare arms painted with ochre hand prints, the torch-staff a raw branch with a stone fire bowl.`
  - Apocalyptique : `The last flame of the wasteland: a jacket of sun-bleached leather with one shoulder pad cut from a wheel hub, the ember inside a chrome exhaust pipe on a steel staff, aviator goggles on his forehead.`
  - Spatiale : `The keeper of the star fire: a sleek white and red space suit with gold stripes, a clear helmet bubble, the ember now a small burning star floating in a ring of gold above the staff.`

## CP4 · Le Fantôme du Virage · légendaire · fond vert

**Mythe : le supporter qui chante encore quand le stade est vide.** Un
fantôme **opaque** (pas transparent : le fond vert le découperait).

```
IDENTITÉ: A tall, slender ghostly supporter with pale lavender-white skin, a gentle mischievous smile, glowing pale blue eyes and long wispy white hair. He wears a long dark purple (#5A2D82) hooded supporter's coat over an old-fashioned white football shirt, dark trousers, and his legs fade into a solid swirl of pale mist at the ankles, fully opaque. Around his neck, falling to his knees, a long hand-knitted football supporter's bar scarf in dark purple (#5A2D82), white and gold (#F2C14E) bars, plain knit with no text and no crest. On his chest, a small round gold medallion shaped like a stamped card. His signature item is an old brass hand lantern with a pale blue flame, held in one hand: keep exactly these colours.
```

- Légendaire : `The ghost who never left the stand turned into a living legend: his coat now of dark purple embroidered velvet with gold piping, the scarf woven with fine gold threads, the lantern of polished gold and glass with a bright pale blue flame. Crowning element: a tall halo-shaped arch of old golden stadium floodlights rising behind his head and shoulders like a crown, readable at thumbnail size. Golden light (#FFC23D) glowing from the lantern and along the gold piping, staying on the body. Floating just above a small round step of dark stone ringed with gold. Gentle, mysterious, friendly face, mouth closed. Fully opaque, not transparent.`
- Pose : `Standing tall facing the viewer, the lantern held up at chest height in one hand, the other hand resting on the scarf, gentle mysterious expression, mouth closed. Correct anatomy: exactly two arms, two hands with five fingers each, no extra or duplicated limbs.`
- Planche, figure « pousse » : `raising the lantern high above his head, close to his body, singing with his mouth wide open`
- Skins :
  - Halloween : `The phantom of the last night of October: a carved-pumpkin lantern with a pale blue flame, small devil horns, a tattered purple and orange cape.`
  - Préhistorique : `The spirit of the first cave: a hooded coat of pale fur and hides, the lantern a stone bowl holding a pale blue flame, bone beads in his hair.`
  - Apocalyptique : `The ghost of the wasteland stand: a dusty leather hooded coat with one shoulder pad cut from a hubcap, aviator goggles in his hair, the lantern rebuilt from a chrome exhaust pipe.`
  - Spatiale : `The ghost of the lost space station: a white and purple space suit with gold stripes, a clear helmet bubble, the lantern a small floating orb of pale blue light.`
